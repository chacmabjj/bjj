Before you request

A few useful answers.

Do I need any Jiu-Jitsu experience?

No. Private coaching works particularly well for complete beginners because Saul can set the pace and explain the fundamentals without the pressure of keeping up with a class.

Can I request a session for my child?

Yes. Choose “Youth / child coaching” in the form. Saul can then discuss age, goals and the practical setup directly with the parent or guardian.

What if I am flexible on date or time?

Use the alternative date and time fields or mention your wider availability in the notes. More flexibility usually makes arranging a session easier.

Where can sessions take place?

Depending on what is practical, sessions can take place at your home, gym, workplace or another suitable venue across Cape Town, the Winelands.

Prefer a conversation first?

Speak to Saul directly.
